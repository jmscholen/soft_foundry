# Deviations From Plan

There was no written plan for this change beyond the request; the three-change plan the maintainer approved ended with the previous change. The design decisions are in `decisions.md`, and the interpretation of the request (machine-wide, across repositories) is stated in `00-intake/assumptions.md` for the maintainer to confirm or correct at merge.

The first delivery read the request too narrowly: it listed processes started as `soft-foundry` and stated, as a limit, that sessions started without it were not listed. Those sessions were the point. The maintainer said so, and the change was reopened: verification, evaluation, attack, review, and learning went back to in progress, the fix was made test-first, and all of them were produced again at the new commit. Affects REQ-PS-001 and adds REQ-PS-009.

One stylesheet rule was added after the first browser screenshot showed short columns wrapping; it is part of the verified commit.

Each deviation records: what changed, why the plan could not be followed, which requirements are affected, and who must approve it.
