# Specification
Translate validated intent and discovery into testable requirements. Cover user behavior, accessibility, authentication, authorization, data, failure behavior, performance, operations, and applicable infrastructure. Requirements must be implementation-independent and traceable. Accessibility requirements follow `.ai/rules/accessibility.md`: at least one `category: accessibility` requirement whenever the change declares `surfaces.accessibility: true`, otherwise a statement of why nothing a person perceives or operates changes. Policy requirements follow `.ai/rules/policy-conformance.md`: at least one `category: policy` requirement whenever the change declares `surfaces.policy: true`, naming what the change collects, shares, retains, or promises differently and which published document and clause covers it, otherwise a statement of why nothing the application's privacy policy, security policy, or terms cover changes.
