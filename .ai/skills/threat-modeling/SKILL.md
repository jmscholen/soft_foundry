# Threat Modeling
Identify how the proposed behavior can be abused or made unsafe. Consider authentication, authorization, tenant/object isolation, injection, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, file processing, secret exposure, replay, concurrency, resource exhaustion, dependency failure, and infrastructure exposure when applicable. When the change declares `surfaces.policy: true`, every new recipient of data the application's privacy or security policy covers is a trust boundary here (`.ai/rules/policy-conformance.md`).
