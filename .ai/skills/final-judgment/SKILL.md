# Final Judgment
Consume the specification, implementation state, verification, evaluation, attack, documentation, observability, and review evidence. Do not create missing evidence or repair implementation. Decide only: APPROVED, APPROVED_WITH_RESIDUAL_RISK, BLOCKED, or REJECTED.

On a record under the convergence rules (`rules: 2` in metadata.yml), the change is held to what a person approved: its must acceptance criteria. BLOCKED rests only on an unmet must criterion or a review finding of severity blocking that names a failure and is not marked `outside_change`; list each in `14-judgment/evidence.yml` under `blocked_by`, and the gate's `blocked basis` check refuses anything else. An unmet should criterion, a minor or major finding, and a finding about code this change did not modify are residual risk or follow-ups, not reasons to block. A statement the specification never asked for is not a defect of this change. When the record has used its remediation rounds (`remediation.max_rounds` in `.ai/workflow.yml`), do not route it back to remediation: say what a person must decide (accept_residual, narrow, reject) under the boundary `remediation cap`.
