# Final Judgment
Consume the specification, implementation state, verification, evaluation, attack, documentation, observability, and review evidence. Do not create missing evidence or repair implementation. Decide only: APPROVED, APPROVED_WITH_RESIDUAL_RISK, BLOCKED, or REJECTED.

On a record under the convergence rules (`rules: 2` or above in metadata.yml), the change is held to what a person approved: its must acceptance criteria. BLOCKED rests only on an unmet must criterion or a review finding of severity blocking that names a failure and is not marked `outside_change`; list each in `14-judgment/evidence.yml` under `blocked_by`, and the gate's `blocked basis` check refuses anything else. An unmet should criterion, a minor or major finding, and a finding about code this change did not modify are residual risk or follow-ups, not reasons to block. A statement the specification never asked for is not a defect of this change. When the record has used its remediation rounds (`remediation.max_rounds` in `.ai/workflow.yml`), do not route it back to remediation: say what a person must decide (accept_residual, narrow, reject) under the boundary `remediation cap`.

`evidence.yml` lists the code-quality phase (`06a-code-quality`) with the other evidence. When it ran, weigh its open major findings as residual risk and name them. When it did not run (the repository's quality gate is off, the risk is low, the track makes it optional, or the record predates it), record its `result` as `not_run` with `commit_sha: null`. A phase that did not run is never recorded as a pass.
